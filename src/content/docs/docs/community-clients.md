We love seeing people build around HQBase. Independent community clients give you more ways to use
your workspace.

## Herald

[Herald](https://github.com/awizemann/herald) is a native macOS email client for HQBase. Visit the
project for current requirements, installation instructions, and releases.

HQBase has tested Herald for compatibility. It is made and maintained by independent community
developers, so its releases, support, and behavior remain in their care rather than HQBase's.
Please review the project and decide whether it is right for your workspace before you connect it.
