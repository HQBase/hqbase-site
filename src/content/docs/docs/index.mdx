Use these guides to install HQBase, give people access to shared mailboxes, connect AI tools, and
keep your workspace running. HQBase runs in your Cloudflare account, so your mail, data, and app
secrets stay there.

## Building or maintaining HQBase?

The [Product reference](/docs/specs/product/) explains behavior that spans several parts of HQBase.
[For maintainers](/docs/maintainers/) covers engineering, staging, releases, and
documentation work.

The public [`HQBase/hqbase`](https://github.com/HQBase/hqbase) repository contains the complete
product source and signed releases.
